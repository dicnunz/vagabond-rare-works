I / Outside the book

Material made to circulate briefly, or not circulate at all. Advertising, early character posters, then prize sheets folded into envelopes.

II / Folded and mailed

The fold is part of the object. Creases here are not damage to erase; for many prize posters they are evidence of how the work reached its owner.

III / Space becomes page

The 2008–2010 Last Manga Exhibition treated the museum as a manga volume. The final objects here are not generic merch: they survive from that spatial work and its limited web shop.
